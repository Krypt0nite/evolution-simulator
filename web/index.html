        Watch evolution happen

        EvoLab is a population of virtual organisms on a petri-dish "plate". Nobody tells them which traits are best: whatever helps them survive and reproduce becomes more common, generation after generation.

        • OrganismsEach teardrop is one organism, pointing where it is heading. It senses what is within its vision, eats, flees, mates and spends energy. It dies of starvation, old age, hazards or predators. Colour shows which original founder it descends from.
        • FoodThe green glow is plant biomass. It regrows over time, richest in patches, and is the prey's only source of energy, so organisms compete for it.
        • Genetic traitsEvery organism carries a genome of heritable genes: speed, vision, body size, maturity age, metabolism, caution and more. Offspring inherit their parents' genes with small random mutations.
        • EvolutionOrganisms whose traits suit the current conditions leave more offspring, so trait averages shift. Chance (genetic drift) shifts them too, especially in small populations. Follow the change in the Live tab and the timeline at the bottom.
