# e6_environmental_change

Stable climate vs a single +1.0 temperature shift at tick 6000 vs a continuously oscillating climate (amplitude 0.8, period 1500).

**Question:** How do populations respond to abrupt vs recurring environmental change — in size, recovery time, thermal optimum and diversity?

Runs: 10 seeds × 3 conditions, 15000 ticks each. Unit of replication = one independent simulation run. Values are mean ± SD [95% t-CI]; d = Cohen's d vs. `stable`; p = Welch's t-test.
