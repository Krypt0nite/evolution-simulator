# e3_resource_distribution

Same total capacity scale; food spread uniformly, in static Gaussian patches, or in patches that relocate every 1500 ticks.

**Question:** How does the spatial and temporal structure of food change population size, movement traits (speed, vision) and diversity?

Runs: 10 seeds × 3 conditions, 15000 ticks each. Unit of replication = one independent simulation run. Values are mean ± SD [95% t-CI]; d = Cohen's d vs. `uniform`; p = Welch's t-test.
