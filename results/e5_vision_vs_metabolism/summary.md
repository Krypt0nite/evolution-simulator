# e5_vision_vs_metabolism

Vision radius fixed at 6, 20 or 45 (vision costs maintenance energy), compared with freely evolving vision.

**Question:** Does better sensing pay for its energetic cost? Where does vision settle when it is free to evolve?

Runs: 10 seeds × 4 conditions, 15000 ticks each. Unit of replication = one independent simulation run. Values are mean ± SD [95% t-CI]; d = Cohen's d vs. `evolvable`; p = Welch's t-test.
