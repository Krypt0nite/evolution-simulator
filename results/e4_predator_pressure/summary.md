# e4_predator_pressure

Prey alone vs prey with coevolving predators (introduced at tick 1500) vs predators with prey speed held fixed (no speed arms race).

**Question:** How does predation change prey traits (speed, size, caution), and can predators persist when prey are free to evolve escape speed?

Runs: 10 seeds × 3 conditions, 20000 ticks each. Unit of replication = one independent simulation run. Values are mean ± SD [95% t-CI]; d = Cohen's d vs. `no_predators`; p = Welch's t-test.
